Our Mission

The Grow Tree Farmers Association is committed to promoting sustainable farming practices, tree cultivation, and environmental conservation in the Savannah Region. We provide resources, training, and support to farmers to enhance agricultural productivity while preserving our natural ecosystem.

Our association focuses on:

  • Sustainable agriculture practices
  • Tree planting and conservation
  • Soil health improvement
  • Pest control management
  • Farmer education and training

About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2010, the Grow Tree Farmers Association began as a small group of farmers in the Savannah Region who recognized the importance of sustainable agriculture and tree conservation. Over the years, we have grown into a respected association with over 500 active members across the region.

Our work focuses on promoting sustainable farming practices that protect the environment while improving farmers' livelihoods. We collaborate with agricultural experts, environmental organizations, and government agencies to implement projects that benefit both farmers and the ecosystem.

Our Achievements

  • Planted over 50,000 trees across the Savannah Region
  • Trained 300+ farmers in sustainable agriculture
  • Established 5 community tree nurseries
  • Reduced soil erosion by 40% in member farms
  • Increased crop yields by 25% through improved practices
